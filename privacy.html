Privacy Policy

Benchmark Sports Calendar · Effective November 20, 2026

The short version

Benchmark does not collect, store, sell or share any of your information. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Calendar links you add. They are saved only on your iPhone. The app downloads your schedule directly from your team's app or league website, the same way your calendar would. No copy is sent to us or anyone else.

Calendar access. Benchmark adds, updates and removes only the events it created in the calendars you choose. It does not read, upload or share your other events.

Notifications. Schedule change alerts are created on your iPhone. No server sends them.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Benchmark is intended for parents and other adults managing family schedules. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Remove a schedule in the app, or delete the app, to erase everything it stored. Events it added to your calendars can be removed from the app before you delete it, or by hand in your calendar.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Contact us here.
